Hi, I'm Xizhi Wang. 你好，我是 Xizhi Wang。

I am a Ph.D. student at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), advised by Prof. Jun Zhang, since 2026. I received my B.Eng. from the School of Electronic Information and Communications at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (2022–2026). My research focuses on multimodal large language models (MLLMs) and personal agents — building AI assistants that see, hear and understand the world the way their users do, remember personal context, and proactively help people in everyday life. I have also worked on 3D digital humans, including facial reconstruction, motion capture and avatar rendering, as well as evaluating audio-video generative models.

我自 2026 年起在香港科技大学（HKUST）张军教授组攻读博士学位，此前于 2022–2026 年就读于华中科技大学电子信息与通信学院，获得工学学士学位。 研究方向为多模态大模型（MLLM）与个人智能体（Personal Agent）： 希望构建能像用户一样去看、去听、去理解世界，记得用户的个人上下文，并在日常生活中主动提供帮助的 AI 助手。 此前也做过 3D 数字人相关工作，包括人脸重建、动作捕捉与数字人渲染，以及音视频生成模型的评测。

Research研究方向

What I'm working on — published work and ongoing projects. 正在做的事情：包括已发表的工作和进行中的项目。

Ongoing进行中

Multimodal LLMs & Personal Agents多模态大模型与个人智能体

Personal AI agents built on multimodal large language models: understanding images, video and audio from the user's daily life, maintaining long-term personal memory, and proactively helping with everyday tasks. I also explore efficient visual understanding of daily experiences, reusable visual representations, and visual question answering for evaluating understanding over time.

基于多模态大模型的个人智能体：理解用户日常生活中的图像、视频与音频， 维护长期的个性化记忆，并在日常任务中主动提供帮助。 同时探索日常经历的高效视觉理解、可复用的视觉表征，以及用于评估跨时间理解能力的视觉问答。

  • MLLM
  • Personal Agent
  • Memory
  • Efficient Perception

Audio-Video Generation & Evaluation音视频生成与评测

Systematically evaluating joint audio-video generative models — visual quality, audio quality and, crucially, cross-modal synchronization and semantic alignment between what we see and what we hear.

系统性地评测音视频联合生成模型：不仅关注画面与声音各自的质量， 更关注两者之间的跨模态同步性与语义一致性——“看到的”与“听到的”是否对得上。

  • Benchmark
  • Multimodal Generation

Wireless Sensing & ISAC无线感知与通感一体化

Bringing deep learning to integrated sensing and communication (ISAC): lightweight neural networks that let base stations recognize targets such as UAVs and birds from radio signals.

将深度学习引入通感一体化（ISAC）：设计轻量级神经网络， 让基站能够仅凭无线信号识别无人机、鸟类等低空目标。
